# 🛒 Shubham Cart Sales Dashboard — Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

**Shubham Cart Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The purpose of this project is to analyse sales performance across different cities and present important business metrics through interactive data visualizations.

This is my **first Power BI dashboard project**, created as part of my learning journey in **Data Analytics and Business Intelligence**.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this dashboard is to transform sales data into meaningful visual insights and provide a simple overview of business performance.

The dashboard helps analyse:

* Total Price
* Total Quantity
* Total Revenue
* Total Orders
* City-wise Price Performance
* City-wise Revenue Contribution
* Geographic Sales Distribution
* Revenue and Quantity Comparison

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard contains four major KPI cards:

### 💰 Total Price

Displays the total value of products/prices available in the dataset.

### 📦 Total Quantity

Shows the total quantity of products represented in the dataset.

### 💵 Total Revenue

Displays the overall revenue generated from the analysed data.

### 🧾 Total Orders

Shows the total number of orders using the Order ID count.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Price by City

An area/line-style visualization is used to compare the total price across different cities.

The dashboard currently analyses:

* Kolkata
* Chennai
* Mumbai
* Delhi
* Bangalore

This visualization helps identify differences in price performance between cities.

### 2. Revenue by City

A donut chart is used to show the contribution of different cities to total revenue.

This provides a quick view of the percentage contribution of each city.

### 3. Geographic City Analysis

A map visualization is used to display the geographical location of the analysed cities.

This provides a geographical perspective of the business data and helps understand where the analysed sales activity is located.

### 4. Quantity and Revenue Comparison

A column chart is used to compare quantity and revenue-related metrics.

This helps provide an overview of the relationship between quantity and revenue.
